🧠 About me

Senior Product Manager with 15+ years of experience across VTEX, iFood and B2W, with a growing focus on AI products and automation.

At VTEX, I act as the technical bridge between support, engineering and product for Brazil's marketplace connectors — debugging APIs, analyzing logs to diagnose complex integration issues, and turning recurring operational pain points into evidence-based backlog decisions.

Over the last year I've been building an ecosystem of AI skills and MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that automate support investigation workflows — from triaging marketplace tickets to surfacing known issues and drafting engineering handoffs — cutting the manual work needed to diagnose and route problems.

I have hands-on technical background (Python, SQL, REST APIs, Postman) and worked directly alongside Data Science / ML teams at iFood and B2W. I'm currently pursuing a postgraduate degree in AI-driven Process Automation Engineering, and I'm looking for opportunities as a PM in AI products, data platforms or automation, where business context and technical depth combine to ship real impact.
